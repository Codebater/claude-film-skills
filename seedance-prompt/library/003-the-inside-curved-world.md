# 003 — THE INSIDE (ours, `curved-world/prompt.txt`)

**Shape:** single generation, one continuous unbroken shot, 15 s, no cuts
**Assets:** 2 (both environment plates, bound by channel)
**Register:** naturalistic documentary coverage of an impossible place

Source lives in the repo at [`curved-world/prompt.txt`](../../../../curved-world/prompt.txt) —
linked rather than copied so it cannot go stale. Excerpts below.

## What it taught

Devices this prompt has that the external exemplars do not:

- **The physics clause** (techniques §8.1) — one governing law stated once, from which every
  downstream behaviour follows without further instruction. The strongest single line in our corpus:
  > `Gravity is local: each landscape pulls toward the surface it sits on, so waterfalls, smoke and clouds each follow their own ground's direction.`
- **Overall-requirements tail** (§8.2) — a closing paragraph re-asserting whole-piece constraints,
  bracketing the shots between a header and a tail. Neither external exemplar does this, and it is
  measurably better than repeating a constraint twice in the middle.
- **Asset binding by channel** (§8.3) — `@Image2 defines the overhead geometry: …` rather than
  "here is a picture".
- **Material truth as a positive** — the defence against a collage look, phrased entirely
  positively:
  > `all of it solid anchored terrain built from real photographic forest, rock, grass, sand and water at every distance and in every part of the frame`
- **Positive-only motion control** — `natural real-time motion speed throughout`, achieving what
  `NOT slow-mo` achieves in 001/002 without a negative.
- **Deep focus stated as a relationship, not a setting** — `the daisies closest to the lens and the
  landmass forty kilometres overhead are both sharp, and distance is expressed through layered blue
  atmospheric haze.` Two named planes plus the mechanism, which is the deep-focus twin of 001's
  telephoto passage.

## What it lacks — the gap that motivated this skill

Comparing it against 001 and 002 shows what our house style was missing. Every item below is now a
required block in [`../references/prompt-schema.md`](../references/prompt-schema.md):

| Missing | Consequence | Now handled by |
|---|---|---|
| Identity string | the protagonist is `a lone man in a white shirt` — a different man is possible at every beat | §1.2 |
| Signature detail | no eye-checkable continuity anchor | §1.3 |
| SCALE block | `forty kilometres overhead` appears once, in prose, and never travels with a noun | §2.1 |
| LOCKS block | non-negotiables are dissolved into the overall-requirements tail | §6.1 |
| Skin / acting blocks | the man has no face direction at all — acceptable here only because he is never in close-up, and a latent failure the moment he is | §4.4, §5.1 |
| Accent doctrine | palette listed as hues (`deep cypress green, warm ivory limestone…`) with no percentages and no named sources | §4.2 |
| Named DP | no operating style invoked or decoded | §4.1 |
| Per-shot sub-schema | timeline ranges carry map, optics, camera and action fused into one paragraph each | Part B |

The prompt works despite these because it is a landscape piece with one distant figure and no cuts —
the four hardest drift classes (identity, geometry across cuts, face, scale between characters)
barely get exercised. It would not survive being a dialogue scene.

## Verdict

Keep §8.1–8.3 as house doctrine; they are genuinely ours and better than the alternatives. Adopt
identity, scale, geometry, performance and the labelled shot sub-schema from 001 and 002. The
synthesis of both is what [`../references/prompt-schema.md`](../references/prompt-schema.md)
encodes.

## Excerpts

```
A lone survivor walks through an endless flowering garden toward a distant thread of smoke, and as
the camera rises behind him the land bends upward on every side and closes overhead, revealing that
he is standing on the inside surface of a colossal hollow world. Photorealistic prestige
science-fiction, one continuous unbroken crane-and-tilt shot.

Style: naturalistic documentary cinematography of an impossible place, patient and matter-of-fact,
the way a wildlife film would cover it. 35mm anamorphic texture with fine film grain and subtle
horizontal highlight streaks. Wide-angle deep focus: the daisies closest to the lens and the
landmass forty kilometres overhead are both sharp, and distance is expressed through layered blue
atmospheric haze. One high hard sun lights the entire world from a single direction; enormous soft
shadows cast by the landmasses overhead drift slowly across the meadow. Palette of deep cypress
green, warm ivory limestone, white and coral flowers, true blue sky and silver water. Clear air,
matte highlights, natural real-time motion speed throughout.

@Image1 defines the paradise environment: …
@Image2 defines the overhead geometry: land, lakes and forest hanging upside down high above the
character, with white clouds drifting through the open space between the two sides of the world.

…

Overall requirements: one continuous unbroken shot with no cuts. The camera stays inside the world
for the entire shot. Every landmass is physically attached to the continuous curved ground, all of
it solid anchored terrain built from real photographic forest, rock, grass, sand and water at every
distance and in every part of the frame. Gravity is local: each landscape pulls toward the surface
it sits on, so waterfalls, smoke and clouds each follow their own ground's direction. Consistent
sunlight, consistent atmosphere and natural real-time motion throughout. Widescreen 16:9 anamorphic
frame. No subtitles.
```
