# 002 — Sofa scene, Mira & Harumin (3 shots, three generations under one bible)

**Shape:** shot series under a shared bible — the same header pasted onto three separate
submissions, assembled in the edit
**Assets:** 7 (4 characters, 2 location faces of one room, 1 spare)
**Register:** photoreal 65mm, naturalistic dialogue scene, sultry comedy

## What it taught

New to the catalog when ingested:

- **The series bible** (techniques §1.1) — the defining lesson. Each shot re-declares its full
  OPTICS, CAMERA and LOCATION MAP because each is its own generation; the header above them is
  byte-identical across all three. This is what buys per-shot re-rolls.
- **The labelled shot sub-schema** — `LOCATION MAP / FORMAT MODE / OPTICS / CAMERA / ACTION TIMING`.
  Adopted wholesale into the house schema.
- **Identity string** (§1.2) with height, hair transition, wardrobe to the feet, and voice register.
- **Signature detail** (§1.3) — the holographic nose strip, `keep in every frame`, restated per shot.
- **Context variant as a delta** (§1.4) — `(no choker at home)`, `NO horns at home`.
- **Voice casting by register** (§1.5) — mezzo-soprano vs. soprano.
- **Two-face location plating** (§2.3) — `@Image 7 (main face) and @Image 1 (reverse face) — same
  room, opposite walls`. The reverse-angle fix.
- **Seating chart** (§2.2) — LEFT / MIDDLE / RIGHT for a fixed ensemble.
- **Accent doctrine** (§4.2) — the 70/20/10 palette with each band naming its physical source.
- **Redundancy budget** (§6.4) — five channels on one constraint in shot 1, everything else once.
- **Declare what is sharp** (§3.3) — `~f/1.4`, `ONLY @Image 3's face`.
- **Fight the centering prior** (§3.4) — `off-centre, never centred` in every shot.
- **Dialogue split across the cut** (§7.2) — one sentence broken over shots 2 and 3 with ellipses
  as pause marks and separate delivery notes for each half.

## Notes and cautions

- The header block order here is the one adopted as the house template: Style → Operating style →
  Light → Colour → Texture → Camera/Optics → Skin → Acting → Composition → Technical → Audio →
  ASSETS → SEATING → Locations.
- Asset numbering is not shared with 001 — Harumin is `@Image 2` here and Haru Min is `@Image 1`
  there. Numbering is per submission; only the identity strings are portable.
- Durations are given as `~5s` / `~4s` in FORMAT MODE **and** as a `0–5s` local timeline in ACTION
  TIMING. Both, not either.
- The shot-1 background constraint is the clearest worked example of the redundancy budget in the
  corpus. Count the restatements before writing your own.
